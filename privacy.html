Contact for privacy questions and requests: software@h2oil.co.uk.

We apply the data-protection law that governs us in the UAE, and, for users in the United Kingdom and the European Economic Area, the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR.

3. Data that stays on your device

  • Every calculator, PRiSM analysis, DFIT and build-up interpretation, simulation and report is computed in your browser or in the app. There is no server-side calculation.
  • Your inputs, client and well details, loaded gauge data, results and preferences are kept in your browser's local storage and databases (in the iOS app, the app's own storage). We cannot see or recover them. Clearing the site's data in your browser, or deleting the app, removes them.
  • Project files and PDF, PNG, CSV and spreadsheet exports are created only when you choose to save or share them, and go only where you put them.
  • Live data (Modbus) connects only to devices you configure, through a bridge program you run on your own network (web) or directly from your device (iOS). Tag values and history stay in your browser's or app's local database.
  • The web version keeps an offline copy of the app and its help pages in your browser (a service worker cache). It contains code and images only, not your data.
  • Some optional features download public code libraries (the 3D engine, a database engine for the historian, a spreadsheet library) from a public content-delivery network the first time they are used. These requests contain no data from you beyond what any web request contains (such as your IP address).

4. Usage analytics (web version)

The web version uses Google Analytics 4, provided by Google, to count how the app is used. A filter in our page sends only page and module names, fixed button and model names and a few interface counters (tab number, scroll depth, seconds on a page). It never sends your inputs, client or well names, file contents, results or text you type or copy.

As with any website using it, Google Analytics also receives technical data from your browser: your IP address (which Google uses to estimate a coarse location and says it does not store), device, browser, screen size, language, the referring page and an analytics identifier stored in a cookie (see section 8). Some events are generated automatically by Google's script and are not covered by our filter.

  • The analytics script is not loaded while you are signed in to an account.
  • The iOS app contains no analytics code.
  • You can block analytics with your browser's privacy settings or a content blocker; every feature keeps working.
  • We are adding an in-app choice to accept or refuse analytics. When it is live, analytics will load only after you accept, and you will be able to change your choice at any time.

5. Accounts and cloud projects (web version, optional)

You can use the whole app without an account. If you create one (accounts are being introduced gradually, starting with invited users), we process the following:

  • Your e-mail address, and a recovery address if you add one. We store them encrypted, with a keyed index so we can find your account, and decrypt them only to send you e-mail. You sign in with a one-time code sent to your e-mail, or with Google or LinkedIn. We never ask for or store a password.
  • The projects you save to the cloud: page inputs, PRiSM analyses, wells, build-up surveillance data, report snapshots and templates, client details you entered and your curve and preset library, plus their version history. Project contents and names are encrypted with a key unique to your account.
  • Account records: when the account was created and last used, your sign-in method, the version of these documents you accepted and when, storage used, and any deletion request.
  • Devices and sessions: a random device identifier, the device name you choose, a general browser family (for example “browser / mobile”, not your full browser details), when each session started and was last used, and a shortened IP address (the first three blocks of an IPv4 address, or the first 48 bits of an IPv6 address).
  • Security log: sign-ins, sign-outs, changes to your sign-in methods or e-mail address, exports and deletion requests, with time, device, shortened IP address and browser family. It never contains your project content or your e-mail address.
  • Short-lived security data: one-time codes (stored only as a keyed hash), sign-in attempts and rate-limit counters, deleted within a day to a week.

From your account page you can export everything we hold about you and your projects, change your e-mail address, and delete your account. A deletion can be cancelled for 7 days; after that we remove your data from our live systems, and it leaves our backups within 30 days. Data on your own devices stays until you clear it. We do not load analytics while you are signed in, and we never use your project data for analytics, advertising or AI training, or share or sell it.

6. Signing in with Google or LinkedIn

These are optional alternatives to the e-mail code. When you choose one, your browser goes to Google or LinkedIn to sign in, and they send us information about you after you agree to share it:

  • Google sends an identifier for your Google account, your e-mail address, whether Google has verified it and, for workplace accounts, the organisation's domain. We ask only for the “openid” and “email” permissions.
  • LinkedIn sends an identifier, your name, a link to your profile photo, your language setting, your e-mail address and whether it is verified. We keep only the identifier and the e-mail address; the rest is discarded straight away.

We use this information only to create your account, sign you in and keep your account secure. We do not post anything, read your contacts, import your profile, or keep access to your Google or LinkedIn account: we store no access or refresh tokens. We do not share this information with anyone, use it for advertising, or use it to develop or train AI models. Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google and LinkedIn learn that you signed in to the Well Testing Suite; they handle that under their own privacy policies. You can unlink Google or LinkedIn on your account page at any time (your e-mail code always still works). Deleting your account removes these links. To also remove the app from your Google or LinkedIn account, use your Google Account permissions page or LinkedIn's data-privacy settings.

7. E-mails we send

If you have an account, we send only service and security e-mails: sign-in and confirmation codes, notices of a new device, changes to your e-mail address or sign-in methods, exports and deletion requests, and warnings before an inactive account is deleted. They are plain text and come from no-reply@h2oil.co.uk through an e-mail delivery service. We do not send marketing e-mail.

9. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with service providers who process it for us under contract and our instructions:

  • our hosting provider (website, server functions, database and encrypted file storage);
  • an e-mail delivery service (account e-mails only);
  • Google, for Google Analytics on the web version;
  • Google or LinkedIn, if you choose to sign in with them (as independent controllers for their own sign-in services).

We may also disclose data where the law requires it, to protect the rights and safety of our users or the public, or as part of a sale or reorganisation of our business (the buyer would be bound by this policy).

10. Where data is stored and international transfers

Account data is stored with our hosting provider in the United Kingdom or the European Union, and encrypted project files in the European Union. We are based in the UAE, and our service providers or their support staff may access data from other countries. Where personal data of UK or EEA users is transferred to a country without an adequacy decision, we use approved safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Google Analytics data is processed by Google, including in the United States, under Google's safeguards. Contact us for a copy of the safeguards that apply.

12. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), secure sign-in cookies that scripts cannot read, encryption of e-mail addresses and of project content and names with a key unique to each account, hashed one-time codes and session identifiers, database rules that stop one account from reading another's data, rate limits, a tamper-evident security log, and backups. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data we will tell you and the authorities where the law requires it.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its use (including analytics and our legitimate interests), receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time. You can use your account page for export, correction of your e-mail address and deletion, or write to software@h2oil.co.uk. We will confirm your identity first (for example with a code sent to your e-mail address) and answer within one month (longer where the law allows).

You can complain to a data-protection authority: in the UAE, the data-protection authority that supervises us; in the United Kingdom, the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk); in the EEA, your local supervisory authority. We would appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first.

14. Data you store about other people (business use)

If you store data about your employer, clients or other people in your cloud projects (for example client contact names), you decide why and how that data is used, and we process it only to provide the service to you. For such business data we act as your processor; a data processing agreement is available on request from software@h2oil.co.uk. Please do not store special categories of personal data (such as health data) in the app.

15. iOS app

The iOS app has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your device. If you open web links from the app, the web pages' own terms apply.

16. Children

The Well Testing Suite is a professional engineering tool for adults. It is not directed at children, and accounts are for people aged 18 or over. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

17. Changes to this policy

We will post any change on this page with a new date and version. If a change is significant, we will tell account holders by e-mail or in the app and, where needed, ask for agreement again at the next sign-in.
